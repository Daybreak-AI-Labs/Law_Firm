Why create a workspace?

A workspace walls off one client or team: its own records and run history, its own uploaded knowledge and customized agents, its own daily spend limit and usage ledger, and its own per-workspace roles. Evidence never mixes between clients, one workspace can never spend another's budget, and any one of them can be suspended without touching the rest. Create and manage workspaces on Tenants.
