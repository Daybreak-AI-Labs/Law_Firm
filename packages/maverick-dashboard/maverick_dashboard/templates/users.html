What each role can do

admin

Runs the platform. Manages users and roles, settings, provider keys and secrets — plus everything an operator and an auditor can do.

attorney

Runs matter work and may certify legal deliverables, but only for matters where they hold an active attorney membership.

operator

Does the work. Starts and cancels runs and records feedback, but cannot certify or release legal work.

auditor

Reviews the record. Reads the audit trail and reports — and nothing else. Can't run work or change anything, so review stays independent.

viewer

Looks, doesn't touch. Sees dashboards and results, read-only.
