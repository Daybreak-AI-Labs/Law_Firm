## Angels & operator-investors (warm, high-signal)

Don't skip these — they write fast and open doors:
- **Security/compliance operators** — CISOs / Heads of GRC at banks/insurers (your buyers, and credible angels).
- **AI-infra founders** — founders of agent/dev-infra companies (and a16z/Sequoia **scouts**, who seeded InfiniteWatch).
- **Fintech operators** — ex-CFOs/controllers, fintech founders for the BFSI/CPA wedge.
- Find them via your design-partner conversations: a champion who loves the product is often your best angel *and* reference.
