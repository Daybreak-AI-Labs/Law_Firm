03
What it is

Not a chatbot. Not a framework. A governed runtime.

Hand Lightwork a goal. A recursive swarm of specialist agents works it in parallel — and every single action passes through one chokepoint before it happens, then lands where a human signs off.

01
Capability
Attenuate-only tokens. An agent narrows its rights, never widens.
02
Policy
Allow, deny, or require a human, bound to every action.
03
Shield
Screens for the dangerous action and the prompt injection.
04
Budget
Hard caps on tokens, dollars, wall-clock, and tools.
05
Audit
A signed, hash-chained entry you can verify offline.

Self-hosted or air-gapped, on your data, no required egress — so even a successful prompt injection can't move data out.
