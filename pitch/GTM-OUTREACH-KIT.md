# Lightwork — Design-Partner & Investor Outreach Kit

> Two motions, run in parallel. **Design partners** create the demand evidence
> that **investors** fund. The fastest path to a round is "we have 2–3 design
> partners and inbound," not more collateral. Use the golden-path demo and the
> security questionnaire as your door-openers.

---

# Part A — Design-Partner Program

## The offer (what you're actually selling them)

A **founder-led, white-glove pilot** on one real workflow, in their environment,
at near-zero cost, in exchange for feedback + a logo/reference if it works.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they get** | One governed agent workflow stood up on *their* data, in *their* VPC/air-gap; founder-level support; influence over the roadmap; preferential pricing locked in. |
| **What you ask** | ~2–4 hours/week of one champion's time for 6–8 weeks; access to one realistic (can be synthetic) workflow + data; a reference/quote if it delivers. |
| **Time-boxed** | 6–8 weeks, one workflow, clear success criteria defined up front. Small, reversible, theirs to keep. |
| **Not** | Not free forever, not a custom build commitment, not production-critical on day one. |

## ICP — who to target first

**Beachhead: BFSI** (highest governance pain, clearest ROI).
- Community / regional **banks & credit unions** ($1B–$50B assets), **insurers**
  (claims/underwriting ops), **fintechs** under bank-like scrutiny.
- **Second wedge: CPA / accounting firms** (the docs→1040 + state-return pipeline).

**Personas (champion → economic buyer):**
- **Champion:** Head of Automation / AI, Head of Ops, a forward-leaning Controller
  or VP Finance, Head of Internal Audit.
- **Economic buyer:** CFO / Controller (finance pack), CISO + Compliance (the
  gatekeepers who kill ungoverned pilots — your security questionnaire is *for them*).
- **Sponsor to win early:** CISO/compliance. If they bless it, the pilot lives.

**Where to find them:** warm intros (your network, advisors, other founders),
LinkedIn (filter by title + industry + company size), industry slacks/communities,
BFSI/AICPA events, and targeted lists (CFOs/CISOs at regional banks).

## Discovery / qualification (first call)

Qualify *out* fast. A good design partner has: (1) a **specific painful workflow**,
(2) a **named human** who owns it, (3) **data they can share** (even synthetic),
(4) a **reason it's urgent now** (audit finding, headcount freeze, a failed
ungoverned pilot). Questions:
- "Walk me through the workflow you'd most want an agent to take on. What happens
  today when it goes wrong?"
- "Last time you evaluated an AI agent internally — what killed it?" *(listen for
  governance/security/audit — that's your wedge.)*
- "Who would have to sign off — security, compliance, legal — and what do they need to see?"
- "If this worked, what would it be worth — hours saved, exceptions caught, risk reduced?"

## First-call flow (30 min)
1. (5) Their world — the painful workflow + what killed past pilots.
2. (8) **The golden-path demo** — deny the wire, require the human, cap the loop,
   catch the tampered record. "This is governance you can prove, in one command."
3. (5) How a pilot works (the offer above) + the security questionnaire hand-off.
4. (5) Scope one workflow + success criteria.
5. (2) Next step + a date. Always leave with a next date.

# Part B — Investor Outreach

## Target fund profile

Pre-seed / seed funds that do **one or more of:** AI infrastructure, vertical AI,
**govtech / regtech / compliance**, **enterprise security**, or
fintech-infrastructure. Check size that fits your raise; partners who've done
**regulated-enterprise** or **dev/AI-infra** deals. Avoid generalist consumer funds.

**Build the list:** start from funds that backed comparable companies (governance,
agent infra, regulated AI, security tooling). For each, identify the *specific
partner* who led those and your **warmest path** to them. A warm intro beats ten
cold emails — map your network and your advisors' networks first.

## Process (run it tight)
1. **Prep the materials** (teaser, deck, demo, proof, data room — see the data-room checklist).
2. **Build a ranked list** (~20–40 funds; tier by fit + warmth).
3. **Batch outreach** over ~2 weeks so first meetings cluster → creates a real
   process and urgency (don't trickle).
4. **First meeting → partner meeting → diligence → term sheet.** Track every fund
   in a simple CRM/sheet (stage, last touch, next step).
5. **Create urgency honestly** — "we're running a focused process; aiming to wrap
   by {date}." Never fake a fake lead.

## What investors will probe (have answers)
- **Why you** (founder-market fit) · **why now** · **the wedge & first 10 customers**
  · **demand evidence** · **the moat** (governance + provable learning + the
  library) · **the ask & milestones the round buys.** The proof/RESULTS and
  security questionnaire pre-empt the "is it real / can it sell to security" doubts.

## The one thing
Spend the next ~4 weeks **~80% on conversations** (design partners + investors)
and ~20% on the few artifacts that close them. You already have more collateral
than most pre-seed companies — the scarce input now is *pull*.
