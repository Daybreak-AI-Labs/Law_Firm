# Bjerken and Day roadmap

This roadmap tracks work for the firm's private practice platform. Public distribution,
marketplace growth, language SDKs, editor plugins, mobile companions, and demonstration
products are intentionally out of scope.

## 1. Client-data readiness

- Classify every matter by sensitivity and privilege.
- Permit model providers per sensitivity class and block disallowed transmission.
- Record provider transmissions in the matter audit trail.
- Encrypt local data and backups, and test restoration.
- Prove that one matter cannot retrieve another matter's documents.

## 2. VA disability practice

- Intake and accreditation, including the current VA Form 21-22a workflow.
- Appeals Modernization Act lanes: supplemental claim, higher-level review, and Board appeal.
- C-file review, evidence development, nexus-letter and DBQ support.
- Rating analysis under 38 C.F.R. Part 4, TDIU, effective dates, and fee compliance.
- Attorney-reviewed evaluation cases for every workflow.

## 3. Matters and clients

- Clients, prospective clients, related parties, and adverse parties.
- Matters with required practice area and jurisdiction fields.
- Conflicts checks and matter-level ethical walls.
- Matter-scoped documents, versions, communications, and approvals.
- Rules-based deadline and docket management.

## 4. State practice workflows

- Parameterized Tennessee, Georgia, and Florida family-law workflows.
- Estate planning, probate, and residential real-estate workflows.
- Jurisdiction-specific authorities, forms, filing systems, and professional-conduct rules.

## 5. Firm operations

- Time entries, expenses, invoicing, and accounts receivable.
- Trust accounting that uses Florida's controls as the minimum ledger standard, with
  Tennessee and Georgia jurisdiction rules layered on top.
- Court e-filing integrations only after the matter and deadline models are stable.

## 6. Reviewed improvement

- Preserve the generated draft and attorney-edited final as separate versions.
- Explain and classify material edits.
- Promote clauses, templates, and instructions only after attorney approval.
- Measure improvement with firm-specific legal safety and quality evaluations.
